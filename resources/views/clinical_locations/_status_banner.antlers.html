It renders only while a closure is in effect or announced: `closing_query` is on, and the closure is permanent or its reopening date has not passed. A closing date in the past reads "is closed", a future one "will be closing on", and a permanent closure says so where a temporary one names its reopening date or says it is closed until further notice. Dates are compared and printed as calendar dates (`format_civil_date`) against today in the display timezone, so a closure starts on its own date in Central time rather than at midnight UTC the evening before.
